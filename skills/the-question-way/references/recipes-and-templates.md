## Hook patterns (two loops, first paid at once)

- "There are two things that decide X. The first is A… but almost everyone gets it wrong."
- "Two questions before you buy X. First: A — and the answer isn't what you think."
- "X has one fix you know and one nobody talks about. The one you know: A. Here's why it's not
  enough."

## Twist lines (turn an obvious answer into a new question)

- "…but it's not what you think."
- "…and this is exactly where people get it wrong."
- "That's the answer everyone gives. It's half of it."
- "That solves it. Until this happens."

## Chain recipe (problem → bigger problem)

1. Problem the viewer clicked for → fast, real solution.
2. "But now…": the problem that solution creates → solution.
3. "And the one nobody checks…" → solution.
4. Final answer + CTA in the same beat, or the hand-off: "the last one is in part 2."

## Stakes that stay honest

Time (season, deadline, a live test running), money (cost of the wrong choice), risk (safety,
warranty, breakage). Real for anything presented as real.

## Review checklist after publishing

1. Write down the timestamp of each drop on the retention graph (measurement: the platforms'
   native analytics).
2. Which loop closed at each drop? Was another loop open?
3. Next video: open a stronger question before that point.
4. Keep a running list of loops that held and twists that worked.
