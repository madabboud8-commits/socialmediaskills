# Source videos

All three are vertical phone videos (360x640 as received), studied from audio transcripts and
frames. Claims made in them (subscriber counts, views, how retention behaves) are the
creators' claims, **unverified** and **verify-quarterly** if ever quoted.

## 1. "The two best ways to keep viewers watching" (Ross, 2:26)

- **Hook:** two ways, answers #1 at once ("opening loops, aka posing questions"), then "but
  people always do this wrong."
- **Credentials** at ~0:09, one sentence.
- **Mistake 1:** one question answered minutes later with something obvious. **Fix:** two
  loops, pay the first immediately ("a little hit of dopamine").
- **Mistake 2:** explaining an answer the viewer already got. **Fix:** "but it's not what you
  think."
- **Way #2: stakes** — dramatic or everyday ("I only have this much time").
- **Ending:** "the second question is now answered, loop is closed, and your interest declines
  right about now," hard cut, CTA to a creator course that "doesn't exist yet" (honest waitlist).
- **Meta:** keeps pointing out "notice how I did this."

## 2. "How to keep viewers watching the whole time" (Ross with Reckless Ben, 1:34)

- Starts mid-sentence; the guest's credentials go on screen as a text overlay.
- Ben: say the problem in the intro, solve it immediately, and the solution creates a bigger
  problem. Ross renames it "close the first loop, open a second immediately." Name: "the Rick
  and Morty method."
- "Constant dopamine of accomplishing things, but they still have to watch." If the viewer
  feels finished, they click away.
- **CTA:** points to the two-hour conversation on their long-form channel.

## 3. "The secret to getting people to watch your entire video" (Ross, 2:26)

- **Hook:** "the secret … from start to end," credentials, names "opening and closing loops."
- **Contrarian:** everyone talks about the hook; "that's level zero."
- **Core law:** the second a loop closes, viewers decline. Examples from his own videos: a
  motorized treadmill run past people; a roofer discovering a pool on a roof. The drop starts
  at the reveal.
- **Fix:** open another loop before the first closes or immediately after; "plot points."
- **Ending:** brags ("I should charge $10,000"), then opens a new loop on purpose ("the other
  way is a personality-driven channel… whole other tangent") and stops. No CTA; the open loop
  is the bait for the next video.
